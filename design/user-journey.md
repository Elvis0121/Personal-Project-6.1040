# User journey

Tinashe has two weeks left on a cottage in Mbare. A forwarded WhatsApp advert offers a cottage in Avondale at US$150. The caption underneath the advert says there is a US$20 for in agent fees. Last time Tinashe paid that kind of fee, the room had a levy that was never on the flyer, and the money for moving was gone.

A cousin sends a Kumba link instead.

1. On search ([sketch 1](ui-sketches.md)), Tinashe sets Avondale and a maximum of US$300. Two cards appear, and both show rent, deposit, and extras before any contact. The Belvedere house is US$400 plus a levy, so it is out. The Avondale cottage is US$150, deposit US$150, water billed, no viewing fee, move-in 3 October, and marked VERIFIED. That label means Kumba checked the advertiser.
2. On the listing ([sketch 2](ui-sketches.md)), the same terms are still there, and there is still no phone number. Tinashe requests an introduction. Kumba does not ask for money.
3. Mrs Dube sees the request on her listings page ([sketch 3](ui-sketches.md)), next to "Confirm still available," "Mark as let," and "Withdraw." She accepts. Tinashe's listing page then shows a number, 0772 429 864. If she had declined, the page would say so and show no number.
4. They talk, Tinashe views the cottage, and they agree. Mrs Dube marks the listing as let, so it leaves search. Deposit and rent are paid between them, not through Kumba.

Tinashe did not pay to learn the real price, did not chase a listing that had already lapsed, and did not hand money to someone Kumba had not checked. The search ended with a landlord's number and a place to move into on the 3rd.
