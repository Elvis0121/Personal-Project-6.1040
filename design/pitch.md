# Application pitch

## Kumba

*Kumba* is Shona for "home." It's where the search ends.

## Motivation

Renting a home in Zimbabwe means paying strangers just to learn what a place really costs, whether it is still available, and whether the person advertising it can actually let it.

## How it works

Kumba is a rental site where landlords list places with everything a tenant needs to decide up front, and tenants search, connect, and agree to rent without paying anyone for access. Rooms, cottages, flats and whole houses are all welcome. Using Kumba is free for tenants.

### Full-cost listings

Every listing shows the rent, the deposit, every extra charge, and the move-in date, and stays visible only while the landlord keeps confirming it is still available. Tenants can rule out a place that is over budget, or already gone, before spending a cent or a bus fare. Landlords get inquiries from people who already know the terms, instead of the same phone calls about price and availability.

### Verified advertisers

When listing, the advertiser shows proof that they can offer the place, such as a rates bill or title deed in their name, or a letter from the owner if they are a caretaker. Kumba checks it and marks the listing as verified. Tenants can tell a real offer from someone selling access to a home that isn't theirs. Honest landlords and caretakers stand out from the adverts that circulate without their consent.

### Introduce and let

A tenant asks to be introduced, and the landlord's number is shared only if the landlord accepts. Once they have talked and agreed, the landlord ticks a box to mark the place as let, and the listing closes. Tenants get a direct contact instead of paying a fee at every step. Landlords keep control of who gets their number, and stop hearing from people about a place that is already let. Deposit and rent are paid directly between tenant and landlord.
